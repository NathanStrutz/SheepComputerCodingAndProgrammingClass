SHEEP does not have school through the holidays - Thanksgiving through New Year's. We also have a winter break in February, a spring break in March, and generally follow the Dorchester County School District for severe weather closing. Expect 27-29 total weeks of classes.

# Who

This class is available to home school, high school students who are 15 years or older, 10th grade and up, and must be officially registered. Register at [sheep.education](https://sheep.education/).

You can [read more about the teachers](about-your-teachers). We may also have an occasional high school or college student volunteer teacher's aid. In recent years, Ethan Siegal has been a volunteer teacher's aid.

:::tip
Students must be 15 or older and in 10th grade or later. See the [note about age restrictions](age-restriction) if you want to register but are below the limit.
:::
